How Forward Propagation Works

Each neuron receives weighted signals from all neurons in the previous layer, sums them with its bias, then applies an activation function to produce its output. The animation shows this computation flowing layer by layer — watch the traveling dots on the connections and the neurons lighting up as values propagate through the network. Thicker, bluer lines carry stronger positive signals; redder lines carry negative signals. Click any connection to edit its weight.
