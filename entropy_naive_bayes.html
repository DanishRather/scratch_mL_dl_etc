How Entropy Connects to Naive Bayes

Naive Bayes is a generative classifier: it models P(X|Y) and uses Bayes' rule to compute P(Y|X). Entropy H(Y) = −Σ P(y) log P(y) measures the uncertainty in the class distribution. After observing features X, the posterior entropy H(Y|X) measures remaining uncertainty. The difference is the information gain — how much the features reduce uncertainty. High information gain features are the most discriminative.
