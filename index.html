How KNN Works

K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN) is a simple algorithm that classifies a new point by looking at the K closest labeled points in the dataset. It measures the distance (using the selected metric) from the query to every training point, picks the K nearest, and assigns the class that appears most often among them. Larger K reduces noise but can blur decision boundaries; smaller K captures local patterns but may overfit.
